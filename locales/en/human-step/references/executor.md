# Execute and verify a packet

## Before changes

Read the assigned packet, applicable AGENTS.md, and concise current state. Establish the role: execute, review, explain, or prepare instructions only. Avoid rereading the historical archive without a reason.

Check the relevant starting object and revision. For Git, inspect branch, full SHA, working diff, and index. Local changes do not authorize reverting them or including them in your commit. Preserve affected originals or hashes where appropriate; use proportionate protection rather than copying the whole repository for each paragraph.

| Observed state | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Starting block/state matches | Apply the agreed action |
| Intended result already exists | Verify its meaning, skip repetition, report |
| Newer version remains compatible | Preserve local work and apply the compatible part |
| Purpose conflicts, anchor is ambiguous, or mandatory data is missing | Pause the affected part; name discrepancy and needed clarification |

Do not reset/checkout over user work to match a base SHA. Do not choose a coincidental short anchor in another method.

## Execution

Apply only the agreed sequence. Preserve comments, formatting, and data outside the explicit change. Do not expand the packet with incidental refactoring or unrelated bug fixes.

If the installed library/UI needs a minimal adaptation preserving intent and scope, make it within authorization and record before/after, reason, and check. Redesign and requirement changes are not compatibility adaptations.

Keep the initial failure visible. Determine whether the change caused it or the baseline reproduces it. Report out-of-scope defects separately. Do not replace a failing run with a successful one without explaining the intervening change and recheck.

Do not fabricate an execution result by copying expected output or reusing an independent calculation intended to check a real run. Preserve artifact provenance.

Do not present a command, calculation, headless simulation, or another session's screenshot as your GUI action. Confirm human physical actions only through their report or available observations. Without a tool, complete accessible work and state what remains.

## Checks

Choose checks for the change's risk: build, reference search, diff, example behavior, GUI, or device readings. Do not add permanent test infrastructure for minor formatting or tests that merely mirror the replacement text.

Check meaning and material boundaries. Use a small independent numerical example when useful; for files inspect real content/version, and for manual actions use an observable criterion.

Reuse previous checks only with version and scope. Do not repeat unchanged checks without cause or attribute changed-code coverage to an older run. Recheck affected behavior or new failures.

Keep temporary harnesses/artifacts outside unintended build/processing inputs. Remove only your own temporary files when appropriate; retain evidence needed for a report. Do not alter build rules or archive structure without assignment.

For comment-only changes, prioritize diff, executable-code preservation, and explanation accuracy. Build/run needs follow the actual change and project instructions.

## Return

Scale shared/templates/en/human-step-result.md to the task. Include:

- Packet and actual starting/final revisions.
- Step statuses and actual files/settings/actions.
- Necessary deviations and reasons.
- Checks, results, and limits.
- Open defects and remaining manual actions.
- Next agreed step and artifact links.

Plan, execution, passed check, user approval, and submission are separate states. Do not mark the whole task complete with a material check unresolved. After a significant stage, update the existing log and current state; do not leave essential information only in the final reply.
