# Human Step Workflow v0.1.5 experience

- Task/stage, date, author/role: [facts]
- Model and source: [known facts or unknown]
- Skill version/edition and mode: [actually read skill; author/execute/review]
- What helped: [observation and result link]
- Difficulty/user correction: [fact; state when none was received]
- Suspected cause: [separate from established facts]
- Proposal: [change, applicable scope, verification]
- Evidence: [relevant log excerpt, packet, version, artifact]

Scale to the stage. Do not repeat the full log, estimate time/tokens without measurement, or change shared instructions automatically from one review. Use the agreed log language.
