# Select layout and cover separately

Use [the neutral layout](../../../../shared/assets/word/base.docx), [neutral cover](../../../../shared/assets/word/cover.docx), and [measured catalog](../../../../shared/assets/word/catalog.json) only after explicit selection. They are cleaned derivatives, not accepted submissions or universal institutional requirements. Their demonstration language does not force report language. A current user-selected sample takes precedence.

Read the [Word preset contract](../../../../docs/word-preset.en.md) for the bounded merge helper, supported cover modes, rendering provenance and limits. Record resource/hash, body/TOC/table styles and independently chosen cover. Do not copy historical names/topics or mix styles silently; select a secondary sample only for a named element. A mutable link is not pinned bytes.

Choose historical examples by requirements, data, evidence and formatting, not score alone. [Adapted cases](../examples/index.md) are review drafts with neutral identifiers and attributed ratings; original documents/quotes/links stay private. No historical acceptance certifies the cleaned preset or current bytes. Keep current private task records separate from release snapshots and never update old snapshots during ordinary work.
