# Licensing and prices

[Русский](licensing.ru.md). Policy prepared on 2026-10-27 from the owner's approved choices.

## Two alternatives

The public route is [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](../LICENSE). Its English text is copied unchanged from the [official download](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0.txt). This guide explains it and does not replace or narrow it. Permitted noncommercial uses are free. The license also expressly permits use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations and government institutions, regardless of funding. Those permissions do not require a paid license.

For commercial uses outside those permissions, the owner selected a separate commercial alternative: [English terms](../COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md), [Russian terms](../COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.ru.md). Individual: USD 10 once for one person. Team: USD 50 once for up to five people. Both cover the major version named in the purchase confirmation, with published updates in that major version. Future major upgrades are optional separate purchases. There is no recurring renewal obligation.

The manifest expression `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0 OR LicenseRef-Workflow-Tools-Commercial` identifies these alternatives; it does not itself confirm a paid purchase. This is source-available software with a noncommercial public license, not an OSI open-source license. Public download does not grant unrestricted business use.

## Ordinary examples

| Situation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| A student's own noncommercial coursework or a hobby task | Free if permitted by PolyForm |
| Use covered by the license's organizational permissions | Free on PolyForm's terms |
| A freelancer uses the plugin for paid client work outside the free permissions | One licensed person; no fee per client or result |
| An ordinary for-profit business uses it for internal work outside the free permissions | Seats for the people using the plugin |
| A client receives a finished report or code without using the plugin | No additional plugin seat for receiving the result |
| The plugin is resold, sublicensed or exposed for unlicensed users to invoke | Outside the standard commercial grant; separate permission needed unless independently permitted by PolyForm |

Count humans using the plugin, not agents or devices. One authorized person may use multiple devices and compatible clients. A team purchase covers up to five identified users, not an unlimited organization. Existing independent permissions under PolyForm always take precedence over these explanatory examples.

## Purchasing is not open yet

Public licensor identity: not supplied. Commercial contact: not supplied. Payment method: not supplied. Seller jurisdiction and sales paperwork: not supplied. These are intentionally not guessed from private profile data. The publisher must complete them and review the sales documents before taking payments. Until then, there is no checkout link and no automatic paid grant.

A future purchase confirmation must identify the licensor and purchaser, terms revision and language, authorized users, covered major version and any included preview, price, payment reference and effective grant. Keep that confirmation with the accepted terms. The current technical preview version is not automatically an entitlement to a future `1.x` release.

The package has no activation server, commercial-use detector or payment collection. No such mechanism is added by licensing. Model-provider subscriptions, tokens, fonts and renderers remain separate. The commercial price includes no personal support or customization unless separately agreed. Final commercial documents need legal review for the actual seller and applicable law; this prepared package is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of directory acceptance.
