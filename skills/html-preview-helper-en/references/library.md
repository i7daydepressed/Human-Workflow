## User rating

**1 means the expected good standard; below 1 means below expectations, and above 1 means better than expected.** A rating applies to a specific result and version. Without feedback, use “not rated”; do not infer a number from praise or transfer it between an image and the HTML. Checks, ratings, and uniqueness are separate considerations.

## Additions

To save a result or feedback outside the package, use the [collection rules](library-additions.md). To include a new author-maintained preset, use the [addition rules](preset-additions.md). Read these only when adding to the library, not for ordinary preset selection.
